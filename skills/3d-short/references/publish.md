# Publishing notes (YouTube, as of Nov 2026 — re-check the linked pages)

**AI label.** "Altered or synthetic content" is required when a viewer could mistake the video for reality (a real person saying something they didn't, edited footage of a real place/event, a realistic scene that never happened). A fully animated 3D cartoon build does not need it. If you use photoreal humans or real places, tick it — the label alone does not reduce reach or monetization. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

**Repetitive / templated content.** YouTube's monetization policy targets channels whose videos differ only superficially and mass-produced template uploads. Vary the story, the problems, the props and the setting every time; record the voice yourself; post at a human pace (the successful channels post ~1 short every 1–2 days). Keep your project files — they prove authorship in an appeal. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

**Length and music.** Shorts can be up to 3 minutes, but a Short longer than 1 minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked worldwide — use only claim-free music (or none). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877

**Money (approximate).** Shorts earn from a shared pool (creators get 45 %); reported rates are roughly $0.03–0.33 per 1,000 views depending on audience country. From 1 Feb 2027 a channel only earns from the Shorts pool in a month where it has ≥10M Shorts views in the last 90 days, and new YPP applicants need 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220

**Other platforms.** TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for videos longer than 1 minute — the 60–78 s build format qualifies. Repost with the same file, native captions off if burned in.

**Title / description.** Title = the transformation ("How a Hole Becomes a Pool"). Description: one plain sentence about the video, credits for any CC-BY assets, no misleading claims.
