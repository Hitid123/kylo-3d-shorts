What happens if you hold in a sneeze?
You pinch your nose and shut your mouth.
But the sneeze still comes, and the air has nowhere to go.
So the pressure in your throat spikes in an instant.
In 2018, a 34-year-old man in England did exactly that.
He felt a pop in his neck.
He had torn the back of his throat, and air leaked into his neck and chest.
Doctors fed him through a tube while it healed.
So when a sneeze comes, just let it out.
