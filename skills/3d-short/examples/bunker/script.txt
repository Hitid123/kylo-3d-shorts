If you dig a hole in your backyard and climb inside, you have a shelter.
But when it rains, the mud walls cave in on you.
So you lower a steel shipping container into the hole.
Cut a hatch in the roof, and add a ladder to climb down.
But after an hour, the air gets thick and hard to breathe.
So you run a vent pipe all the way up to the surface.
Add a lamp, a bed, and enough canned food for a month.
Congratulations, you have built a bunker.
